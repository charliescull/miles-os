# OS Idea Brain Dump

## Personal knowledge capture and organization

- Replicate the usefulness of a notes app inside the OS.
- Allow notes to be filtered and automatically sorted into categories.
- Understand the user's writing style, shorthand, and recurring patterns over time.
- Preserve quick-drop notes on mobile until they can be processed and uploaded.

## Wardrobe and outfit intelligence

- Create a more creative way to organize a wardrobe.
- Add photos of clothing items and maintain an inventory of what is owned.
- Make it easy to assemble outfits visually, potentially through a Tony Stark-style draggable mainframe interface.
- Support sorting, discovery, and outfit creation from existing possessions.

## Pantry, recipes, and nutrition intelligence

- Maintain an inventory of pantry and food items.
- Combine available ingredients to discover recipes and identify useful grocery purchases.
- Consider nutrition, macros, flavor profiles, cooking technique, and personal goals together.
- Use contextual food knowledge, such as:
  - Capsaicin may support metabolic goals, but should not automatically be added to every meal.
  - Sourdough may be preferable to cereal in some contexts while still serving as a convenient carbohydrate.
  - Low-fat marinara and cottage cheese can act as a flavorful, higher-protein binder with ground beef.
  - Garlic and herb seasoning can improve a lemon-pepper grilled chicken meal when added to the pan beforehand.
- Evolve from static tracking toward an assistant that teaches better combinations and cooking decisions.

## Proactive reminders and notifications

- Create creative, effective reminders and push notifications without excessive Vercel or cron costs.
- Support different notification types for upcoming events, week-long goals, birthdays, and other personally meaningful deadlines.
- Make reminders persistent and behavior-aware without becoming noisy or annoying.

## Mobile command center

- Make the mobile experience substantially more complete.
- Seamlessly switch among schedule, wardrobe, pantry, food, and macro-tracking areas.
- Provide a fast capture surface for notes that queues memory locally until it can be uploaded and processed.
- Support initializing AI agents and Closed Feedback Loop workflows from the phone, with execution continuing on the desktop.

## Visual and information architecture refresh

- Shift the overall visual style toward a lighter-toned interface.
- Use red and green consistently to communicate decreases/increases or negative/positive movement.
- Consolidate sections that are used infrequently so the primary workflows remain focused.
- Preserve the expressive, high-information feel while improving clarity and everyday usability.
